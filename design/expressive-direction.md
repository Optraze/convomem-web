Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: a bespoke premium brand campaign hero artwork for ConvoMem, a customer-memory and conversation-intelligence platform. Create a highly art-directed physical studio sculpture, not a website mockup. Subject: a long continuous electric ultramarine blue satin ribbon, wide and substantial like a folded sheet of paper, curling in a beautiful architectural upright loop that gathers a fan of many fine warm-white paper sheets at its base. The blue ribbon has an elegant rounded rectangular cutout through its center like a speech balloon, subtly connecting the ideas of conversations and an enduring archive. The sculpture should have a memorable confident silhouette, beautiful real materiality, slight brushed-metal edge reflections, fine paper grain, and believable crisp contact shadows. Place three small warm ivory porcelain conversation tokens around its base: a handset symbol, an envelope symbol, a simple speech balloon symbol, abstract embossed shapes, no text. Composition: square canvas, sculpture fills about 80% of frame, asymmetric editorial composition with the tall loop slightly right of center, soft sculptural paper sheets fanning toward lower left. Background seamless soft periwinkle light-blue studio cyclorama, colors around #dce5ff, with bright daylight from upper left. Palette strictly electric cobalt #294bff, warm white, pale periwinkle, small restrained polished silver accents. High-end commercial CGI photographed with a medium format camera, sophisticated tactile materials, sharply composed, physically plausible, joyful and visually bold, collectible design object, premium brand design studio quality. No gradients used as decoration, no glowing orbs, no particles, no network lines, no floating dashboard cards, no people, no green, no purple-magenta neon, no logos, no text, no watermarks, no typography. Output a polished square editorial campaign image.
