# Phone and small-screen layout

The feature catalogue switches at 1000px from desktop tabs to five inline category disclosures. Each row opens its own details. Only one is open at a time; collapsing a preceding category preserves the tapped row's screen position. The same 40 feature records remain in the server-rendered document. There are no nested feature accordions or wrapping category pills.

Customer-story channel controls move into the example card below its quote on small screens. The quote reserves enough space to keep the controls stable. Repeated explanatory content is removed from the stacked phone layout. Pricing uses a single selected plan and a compact underline selector, with an explicit compare-all option.

The complete original film remains available in both hero and expanded views. Its surface is a play/pause target, with a visible play affordance while paused. Sound defaults to muted. One 155-second mixed AAC soundtrack replaces separate music and voice playback; HTML animation uses the native media clock. Pause holds the audio and active animations, and seeking resumes the matching position.

Browser validation covers 320–1000px compact layouts, the unchanged desktop tabs, one-open-category behavior, position preservation, nearby story controls, pricing selection, and full-film interaction. Reduced motion disables automatic playback and disclosure transitions.
